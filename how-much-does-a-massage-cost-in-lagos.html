What determines the price of a massage?

Several factors can affect what a massage costs in Lagos. Understanding these factors makes it easier to compare different providers.

Treatment type

Deep tissue, relaxation and couples treatments may have different prices.

Duration

A longer treatment will generally require more time and may cost more.

Spa experience

Facilities, ambience, location and additional services can influence pricing.

Is a more expensive massage always better?

Not necessarily. Price alone does not tell you whether a massage is the right choice for you.

Consider the overall experience, treatment offered, professionalism, cleanliness, location, customer feedback and whether the service matches what you are looking for.

What should I ask before booking?

  • What type of massage is available?
  • How long does the treatment last?
  • What is the current price?
  • Does the price include any additional services?
  • Do I need to book in advance?
  • What are the available appointment times?
