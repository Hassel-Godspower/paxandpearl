What Does Massage Have to Do With Recovery?

Recovery is not only something that happens after intense exercise. For many professionals, recovery also means creating opportunities to step away from prolonged sitting, work pressure, repetitive routines and the constant demands of everyday life.

Massage can become one part of that routine. It does not replace medical care, exercise, good sleep, appropriate workstation setup or healthy lifestyle habits. Instead, it can complement them by creating intentional time for relaxation and body care.
