Who We Are

A wellness destination in the heart of Festac Town

Pax & Pearl Body Works is a massage, spa and beauty-care destination located in Festac Town, Lagos, Nigeria. We provide treatments designed to help clients relax, care for their bodies and enjoy a more intentional wellness experience.

Our services span massage, facials, body treatments, waxing, pedicure and spa experiences. This makes Pax & Pearl a convenient destination for clients looking for massage and personal-care treatments in Festac and the wider Lagos Mainland area.
