What determines the price of a massage?

Treatment type

Deep tissue, relaxation, aromatherapy and other treatments can have different prices.

Duration

A longer appointment will generally cost more than a shorter treatment.

Location

Spa prices can differ between areas of Lagos and between different types of establishments.

Is a more expensive massage necessarily better?

Not necessarily. Price is only one factor when choosing a spa. You should also consider the treatment itself, professionalism, cleanliness, location, customer experience and whether the service matches what you actually want.

Reviews can also help you understand how previous customers experienced a business. Pax & Pearl's official website currently displays hundreds of Google reviews alongside customer feedback. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7}

What should I ask before booking?

  • How long is the treatment?
  • What exactly is included?
  • What pressure or technique is used?
  • Is the displayed price still current?
  • Do I need to book in advance?
  • Where exactly is the spa located?
