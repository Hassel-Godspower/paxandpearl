What should I expect during a massage appointment?

Your experience will depend on the treatment you choose and the spa you visit.

Before your treatment, communicate your preferences, including the areas you'd like the therapist to focus on and the level of pressure you are comfortable with.

If something feels uncomfortable during a massage, communicate with your therapist so the treatment can be adjusted appropriately.

Why choose a local massage spa?

A nearby spa can make massage easier to incorporate into your routine. You spend less time travelling and can choose appointment times that fit more naturally into your schedule.
