What Is the Best Massage for Someone Who Sits at a Desk All Day?

Long hours at a desk can leave people feeling physically tense. Learn how massage can fit into a self-care routine for busy professionals.

Working at a desk for several hours a day can mean spending long periods in the same position. Some people notice tension around the shoulders, neck, back or other areas.

Massage may be one part of a broader self-care routine, alongside regular movement, appropriate workstation setup, rest and other healthy habits.

There isn't one massage that is best for every desk worker. Some people prefer relaxation massage, while others may ask about a more focused deep tissue treatment. Your choice should depend on your comfort, preferences and individual circumstances.

Why do desk workers seek massage?

People may seek massage after long periods of sitting because they want to relax or address feelings of muscular tightness.

Neck & shoulders Common areas people may feel tension after extended desk work.
Upper back Massage may be chosen as part of a relaxation and self-care routine.
Lower back People who sit for long periods may also seek general bodywork and relaxation.

Is deep tissue massage good for desk workers?

Some desk workers may prefer deep tissue massage because they want a more focused massage experience. However, deeper pressure isn't automatically better for everyone.

If you're unsure, discuss your preferences with the spa before your appointment.

How often should a desk worker get a massage?

There isn't a universal schedule that works for everyone. Some people book occasionally when they want to relax, while others incorporate massage into a regular wellness routine.

Your schedule, budget, preferences and individual needs should all be considered.

Don't forget the basics

Massage shouldn't be the only strategy for managing the effects of long periods at a desk.

  • Take regular movement breaks.
  • Pay attention to your workstation setup.
  • Change position throughout the day.
  • Make time for rest and recovery.
  • Seek professional medical advice for persistent pain.
