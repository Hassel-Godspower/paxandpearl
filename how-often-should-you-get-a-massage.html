How Often Should You Get a Massage?

There is no single massage schedule that works for everyone. Here's what to consider when deciding how often massage should be part of your self-care routine.

One of the most common questions people ask after discovering massage is: how often should I get one?

The answer depends on why you are getting a massage, the type of massage you choose, your lifestyle, your preferences and how your body responds to treatment.

Is there a perfect massage schedule?

No. Some people enjoy an occasional massage when they need time to relax, while others incorporate massage into a regular wellness routine.

Rather than following a rigid schedule, consider your personal goals and how you feel before and after treatment.

Your lifestyle

Your work, physical activity and daily routine can influence how frequently you want a massage.

Your goals

Someone seeking occasional relaxation may have a different routine from someone seeking focused massage for muscular tension.

Your comfort

Your personal response to massage is another factor worth considering when planning future appointments.

How often should you get a relaxation massage?

If your primary reason for getting a massage is relaxation, you can schedule treatments according to your personal preferences, availability and budget.

Some people book when they feel they need time to unwind, while others enjoy making massage part of a regular self-care routine.

How often should you get a deep tissue massage?

Deep tissue massage can be more intense than a relaxation massage, so frequency should be considered based on the individual's needs, comfort and response to treatment.

If you are considering massage because of ongoing or significant pain, speak with a qualified healthcare professional rather than relying on massage alone.

Can massage be part of a regular self-care routine?

Yes. For many people, massage is simply one component of a broader approach to relaxation and personal wellbeing. It can be combined with adequate rest, physical activity, hydration, healthy eating and other habits that support general wellbeing.

When should you talk to a healthcare professional?

If you have a significant injury, persistent pain, a medical condition or another health concern that could affect massage, speak with an appropriate healthcare professional before treatment.
