Before Your Appointment

Prepare for a smoother waxing appointment.

Allow appropriate hair growth

Hair generally needs enough length for the wax to grip effectively. Your therapist can advise you based on the area being treated.

Tell your therapist about sensitivities

Let the therapist know about relevant skin sensitivities or products you currently use before beginning your treatment.

Avoid rushing afterwards

Give yourself time after the appointment and follow any aftercare guidance provided by your therapist.
