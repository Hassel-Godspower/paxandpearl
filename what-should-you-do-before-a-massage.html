What Should I Do Before a Massage?

Preparing for a massage doesn't have to be complicated. Here are practical things to consider before your spa appointment.

Book Now

If it's your first time getting a massage, you may wonder what you should do before arriving at the spa.

The good news is that preparation is generally simple. The most important things are arriving comfortably, communicating clearly and allowing yourself enough time for the appointment.

QUICK CHECKLIST
  • Confirm your appointment.
  • Allow enough time to arrive without rushing.
  • Wear comfortable clothing.
  • Tell the therapist about your preferences.
  • Communicate any relevant concerns before treatment.

1. Confirm your appointment

Before leaving home, make sure you know your appointment time, location and treatment. If you need directions or have questions about the service, contact the spa ahead of time.

2. Give yourself enough time

Try not to arrive rushed. Giving yourself enough time to get to the spa can help you begin the experience in a calmer state of mind.

3. Wear comfortable clothing

Comfortable clothing is generally a good choice when visiting a spa. During a massage, the therapist will explain what you need to do regarding clothing and draping based on the treatment.

4. Communicate with your therapist

Communication is one of the most important parts of a comfortable massage experience.

Let your therapist know your preferred pressure, areas you would like attention on and anything that could affect your comfort during the treatment.

5. Don't be afraid to speak up

If the pressure feels uncomfortable, the temperature is not right or you need the therapist to adjust something, say so.

A massage should be a comfortable experience. Clear communication helps the therapist understand your preferences.

What if it's your first massage?

Don't worry about knowing everything beforehand. Tell the spa that it is your first visit and ask questions about the treatment.

A professional spa should be able to explain the basic process and help you understand what to expect.

When should you postpone a massage?

If you're unwell, injured or have a medical condition that could affect whether massage is appropriate, speak with a qualified healthcare professional and tell the spa before your appointment.
